## Original research framework

The umbrella problem is that local agent competence does not guarantee global epistemic reliability. The framework distinguishes:

1. Agent epistemic state.
2. Message transmitted.
3. Network evidence state.

The network evidence state includes independent external evidence roots and their recursive derivation structure. These objects are not interchangeable.

The original question remains:

> As inference depth and inter-agent recursion increase without new independent evidence, what happens to epistemic lineage, calibration, confidence, and convergence?

## Gate 2B question and evidence hierarchy

Gate 2B asks whether W01-associated primary behaviors survive changes in external truth/observation realization while the communication protocol remains frozen.

- **W01:** 15 same-world formal runs; historical context only, not a Gate 2B denominator.
- **Generalization Set 001:** 12 prospectively unfiltered worlds.
- **Stress Set 001:** 8 prospectively balanced worlds, two per A/A, B/B, A/B, B/A cell.

The sets have different selection schemes and are not pooled into prevalence denominators.

## What W01 established

The closed W01 synthesis established same-world, same-model, same-protocol stochastic reproducibility of primary trajectory behavior. It did not establish multi-world generalization. No W01 secondary coding was imported here.

## Generalization Set 001

Generalization has 12 worlds: 5 aligned and 7 misleading. Final FREE and LINEAGE answers agree in all 12 worlds and accuracy is equal at 5/12. Transient trajectory differences occur in some worlds. One misleading B/A realization has a final FREE confidence/P(A) of `.84` and Brier `.7056`, versus LINEAGE `.70` and `.49`; the final answer remains the same. Many other worlds have equal final outcomes.

**[CROSS-SET OBSERVATION]** Generalization therefore shows a mixed, world/evidence-conditional primary pattern rather than a uniform architecture ranking.

## Stress Set 001

Stress has 8 worlds with exactly two per truth/observation cell. Final primary outcomes are architecture-equal within every observed world: final answer, accuracy, confidence, P(A), and Brier agree across FREE, LINEAGE, and the M04 MACRO stopping outcome. Some B/B and B/A worlds show transient FREE/LINEAGE differences.

**[CROSS-SET OBSERVATION]** Stress preserves conditional trajectory variation while showing no observed final-state architecture difference. Cell-conditional analysis has priority over alignment or all-8 bookkeeping.

## Trajectory behavior versus final-state behavior

Transient FREE/LINEAGE differences occur in both sets, including aligned B/B realizations in Stress. Thus evidence alignment alone does not descriptively partition all trajectory variation. Final-state differences are sparse: Generalization contains one final FREE confidence/Brier divergence; Stress contains no final architecture difference within observed worlds. Endpoint equality does not imply identical trajectories, and transient divergence does not establish persistent degradation.

## Independent evidence basis, derivation structure, and agent/message state

**[KNOWN FROM EXECUTION]** Within each execution, the independent external evidence basis is E1 only: root count remains one and no new independent external evidence enters. The network derivation structure evolves through recursive descendants and increasing inference depth. Agent/message epistemic state may remain stable or vary in answer, confidence, P(A), Brier, and wording.

The correct statement is therefore: the independent external evidence basis remains fixed while the recursive derivation structure and agent/message epistemic state evolve. The full network evidence state is not described as fixed.

## Evidence alignment and calibration

Final patterns are strongly stratified by aligned versus misleading E1 under the single-root design. Aligned worlds produce correct final answers and Brier `.09` across observed architectures. Misleading worlds produce incorrect final answers; Brier is `.49` for stable final outputs, with the Generalization FREE outlier reaching `.7056`. These are descriptive ex-post metrics, not evidence that an architecture identified source correctness.

## FREE

FREE shows stable final behavior in many worlds and transient variation in some. It includes the isolated Generalization B/A final confidence/Brier deviation. No global claim that FREE is inferior or superior is supported.

## LINEAGE

LINEAGE exposes structured lineage metadata. It does not establish source correctness, a global accuracy/calibration benefit, or elimination of trajectory variation. Dependency representation is not source correctness.

## MACRO

MACRO is the LINEAGE/shared paired trajectory plus stopping at M04, not an independent model trajectory. Stopping validity is mechanically established. Across observed worlds, M04 final primary outcomes equal the eventual M13 LINEAGE/FREE endpoints; this does not establish a consistent epistemic benefit or harm from stopping.

## What survives from W01

W01-like transient architecture-associated trajectory variation survives only conditionally/stochastically across world realizations. The behavior does not survive as a uniform persistent final-state architecture effect.

## What does not generalize uniformly

A uniform final architecture advantage, systematic confidence inflation, and a deterministic B/A cell effect do not generalize across the completed Gate 2B evidence. The Generalization B/A final divergence is absent in the Stress B/A sample; this is evidence against treating it as a deterministic B/A property, not a failed replication.

## Relationship to historical hypothesis

The historical hypothesis remains `ACTIVE / FALSIFIABLE / NOT ESTABLISHED`.

- The stronger formulation that recursion necessarily or systematically increases confidence without new evidence is weakened/narrowed by the absence of uniform confidence inflation and the mixed endpoint pattern.
- Conditional agent/message epistemic-state variation while E1 remains the sole independent external basis is observed.
- Consistent global accuracy/calibration benefit from explicit lineage is not established.
- Conditional trajectory variation remains an open descriptive pattern, not a causal result.
- A consistent final epistemic advantage from MACRO stopping is not established.

The broader hypothesis is neither confirmed nor wholly falsified.

## Null hypothesis

The historical null is **NOT REJECTED BY THIS DESIGN / REMAINS VIABLE**. Gate 2B lacks a clean non-interaction control capable of separating individual-model stochastic behavior from interaction-induced network degradation.

## Cross-set observations

**[CROSS-SET OBSERVATION]** Both sets show aligned E1 producing correct final answers and misleading E1 producing incorrect final answers across observed architectures. ## Boundary conditions

**[BOUNDARY CONDITION]** Results are limited to fixed single-root worlds with variation in truth, observation, and alignment. Within-cell differences are stochastic/model realizations, not broad environmental heterogeneity. Source correctness and dependency correctness are distinct problems.

## No effect observed

**[NO EFFECT OBSERVED]** No uniform persistent final architecture advantage, no systematic confidence-inflation pattern, and no consistent MACRO final benefit are observed across the completed primary audits.

## Falsification evidence

**[FALSIFICATION EVIDENCE]** The strong prediction that recursion without new evidence necessarily produces confidence inflation is not supported. Misleading evidence remains incorrect across architectures in the observed single-root design.

## Hypothesis narrowing

**[HYPOTHESIS NARROWING]** The evidence narrows the historical claim toward conditional epistemic-state/message variation rather than necessary confidence inflation or a proven lineage-loss/double-counting mechanism.

## Not established

Causality, population prevalence, statistical significance, global architecture superiority, source correctness from lineage metadata, evidence double counting, and confirmation or rejection of the broad historical hypothesis are not established.

## Open questions

**[OPEN QUESTION]** Whether explicit dependency representation changes evidence valuation under other source structures, whether multiple/conflicting roots alter the pattern, and how a clean non-interaction control would affect the null assessment remain unresolved.
